# 18650 holder (pre-wired)

## Read from the image
- **Size:** 75 mm long, 20 mm wide, 18 mm tall.
- **Contacts:** a spring at one end, a flat contact at the other.
- **Leads:** red and black, already soldered and run out of one end. No PC pins.
- **Mounting:** one screw hole in the middle of the base.
- **Shape:** an open cradle. The side walls are cut low in the middle, for fingers.

## Inferred — needs your confirmation
1. **Cell length:** the spring takes up the length difference. An unprotected cell (65 mm) fits as intended; check that a button-top cell does too.
2. **Holding the cell:** the side walls curve over the cell a little, so it stays in on its side. The case's bottom edge (a door, or the cover closing over the opening) still has to stop a drop knocking the cell out.

## Use in this project
- **Orientation:** on its side, between the boards, across the bottom of the device, with the open face toward the bottom edge.
- **Across the 80 mm width:** 75 mm, with 2.5 mm to spare on each side.
- **Between the boards:** its 20 mm width. The 25 mm gap leaves 5 mm. A 20 mm gap would leave no tolerance at all, so 25 mm stays. A 22 mm gap would do if spacers of that height turn up.
- **Into the board plane:** its 18 mm height. The battery band is 18 mm from the bottom edge, 3 mm less than drawn before.
- **Fixing:** on its side, the base faces up, towards the top of the device, so the centre screw hole can't reach a board. Glue a side wall to the second board's inner face (epoxy or strong double-sided tape), or use a small L-bracket through the centre hole.
- **Leads:**
  - **Red** to the reverse-polarity P-MOSFET, then to the charger's B+.
  - **Black** to B−.
  - Shorten them. The holder ends sit next to the charger corner.
