FH1 Boards

The handheld as two stacked perfboards: screen and controls on the main board's front, the S3 mini board and the other modules on its back, the 18650 between the boards at the bottom. Nothing is wired yet, so this page shows where things go. Hover or tap a part to see what it is; pick a group to see only its parts.

2 × 80 × 120 mm perfboard (1.2 mm), 30 × 42 holes 2.8" IPS 320 × 240, SPI gap 25 mm (metal spacers, 20 + 5) 18650 loaded from the bottom edge about 41 mm thick, display socketed
Show

Between the boards: the gap is 25 mm (20 + 5 mm spacers). It holds the cell, the speaker, the 2.4 GHz LoRa radio and the space reserved for a camera coprocessor, all hanging from the second board. Only the two antennas and the camera window stay on the outer face. The battery holder takes the bottom corners, so the bottom pair of spacers sits just above it (two M3 holes drilled through both boards).

The AO3401 adapter boards

Four identical boards: an AO3401 P-MOSFET (SOT-23) on a CNIM SOT-23 → SIP3 adapter, its three pins in one row at 2.54 mm. The AO3401's SOT-23 pins are 1 = gate, 2 = source, 3 = drain, and the adapter brings pad n to hole n. Check the adapter's numbering against its silkscreen before soldering, and meter each FET once mounted: gate to source should read open both ways, and the drain–source diode should conduct from drain to source. Every gate gets a 100 kΩ resistor (1/4 W, from stock) as drawn.
